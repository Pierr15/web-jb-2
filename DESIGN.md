# DESIGN SYSTEM: BLUE COMPUTER

## 1. Brand Identity & Product Vision
Blue Computer is a high-performance computer hardware and peripherals store.
The visual language rejects generic AI templates (e.g. purple/indigo gradients, floating glass orbs, soft generic SaaS look).
Instead, it embraces **Technical Hardware Blueprint & Industrial Precision**:
- Sharp borders with deliberate technical grid alignment
- Hardware spec-dense data tables and monospaced part numbers
- High contrast, dark technical backdrop with bold cobalt/electric blue highlights
- Purpose-driven micro-interactions

## 2. Palette
- **Canvas Base**: `#0A0D14` (Deep Circuit Obsidian)
- **Surface Elevation 1**: `#111622` (Industrial Charcoal)
- **Surface Elevation 2**: `#182030` (Milled Metal Panel)
- **Technical Border**: `#243046` (Precision Wireframe border)
- **Primary Cobalt**: `#0055FF` (Electric Circuit Blue)
- **Primary Hover**: `#2B72FF`
- **Text Main**: `#F1F5F9` (High-contrast slate white)
- **Text Muted**: `#94A3B8` (Technical annotation slate)
- **Status Amber**: `#F59E0B` (Low stock warning)
- **Status Green**: `#10B981` (In-stock / Ready status)
- **Status Red**: `#EF4444` (Out of stock / Alert)

## 3. Typography
- **Headings & Spec Numbers**: Geist Mono / JetBrains Mono (monospaced technical clarity)
- **Body & Copy**: Geist Sans / Inter (clean, high legibility for product descriptions)

## 4. Components & Dials
- **ENERGY Dial**: 2 (Sharp, focused, functional tech equipment feel)
- **RHYTHM Dial**: 2 (Balanced technical modules with spec comparison highlights)
- **MOTION Dial**: 1 (Fast, mechanical 150ms-200ms transitions, no dizzying floats)
